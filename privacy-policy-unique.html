Privacy Policy

Unique Requisites Chrome Extension — Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Unique Requisites does not collect, share, or sell any data. All processing happens locally in your browser.

Data accessed

When you click “Show requisites” on a player page of the lixog.com admin panel, the extension requests the list of the player's payment account numbers from the same admin panel using your existing browser session. The data is displayed in the extension so you can copy it. It is never sent to any other server.

Data stored locally

The last retrieved list (player ID and payment account numbers) is saved on your device using Chrome's built-in storage so it is still visible after the extension window is closed. You can delete it at any time with the “Clear” button, or by removing the extension.

Third-party services

None. The extension only communicates with lixog.com.

No tracking

The extension contains no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting, and no telemetry of any kind.
